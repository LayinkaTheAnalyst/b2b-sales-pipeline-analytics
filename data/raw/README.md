# Raw Data

This folder contains the original CRM Sales Opportunities dataset published by Maven Analytics.

The source files are preserved in their original form. No corrections, transformations, or derived fields have been applied.

## Files

- `accounts.csv` — customer account characteristics
- `products.csv` — products, product series, and suggested prices
- `sales_pipeline.csv` — sales opportunities, stages, dates, and deal values
- `sales_teams.csv` — sales agents, managers, and regional offices
- `data_dictionary.csv` — definitions of the original fields

## Source

[Maven Analytics — CRM Sales Opportunities](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/crm-sales-opportunities)

Accessed for an independent portfolio analysis.
